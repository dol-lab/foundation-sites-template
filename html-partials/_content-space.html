A Space

A post and the sidebar, laid out as defaultspace prints a Space. A post is a .card with an avatar, a header, the content and a footer of chips and avatars; themed with --f-color-theme on a card tints just that card. The sidebar holds tabs, the main menu and accordions of <details>. Their layout comes from this library, some details from defaultspace's own CSS.
